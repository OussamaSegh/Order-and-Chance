A series of books is a city: each tome is a district, and the streets between them are the theorems that one book
proves and another one uses. This volume is the map of that city, generated directly from the sources of the
books themselves.

It shows the shape of the series and the dependency map of which tome leans on which, collects the notation shared
by all the tomes, and lists, tome by tome and chapter by chapter, every named theorem with the number it carries.
It follows the threads between the books, names the results the rest of the series relies on most, and ends with
an alphabetical index of every named result, from Abel's summation by parts to Zorn's lemma.
