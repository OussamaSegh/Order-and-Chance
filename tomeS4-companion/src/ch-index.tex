\chapter{Index of Named Results}\label{ch:index}

Every numbered result that carries a name in its tome, in alphabetical order of the name (ignoring an initial
``the''), with its kind, number and tome. A name that appears several times belongs to several results: a theorem
often comes with its corollary, and some theorems are proved twice, in different tomes, by different methods.
