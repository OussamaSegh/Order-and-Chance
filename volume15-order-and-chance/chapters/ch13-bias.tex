\chapter{When order wins}\label{ch:oc-bias}\label{ch:bias}

Chebyshev's bias is proved here as a computation of a logarithmic density. The headings name the hypotheses: the generalised Riemann hypothesis for $\chi_{-4}$, and linear independence over $\Q$ of the positive ordinates of its zeros. Volume~6 proves the identity that reduces the race to $\psi(x,\chi)$, and quotes the percentage. The torus integral is this chapter's. Maier's irregularities are Chapter~\ref{ch:gaps}. The bias of Lemke Oliver and Soundararajan for consecutive primes is stated, not proved, and the heading that states it says so.
