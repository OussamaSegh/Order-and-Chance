\chapter{Random groups in arithmetic}\label{ch:oc-classgroups}\label{ch:cohenlenstra}

The Cohen--Lenstra measure on finite abelian $p$-groups, its moments, and the heuristic that a class group is a random group for that measure. The measure and the moment calculations are proved. The heuristic is stated as a heuristic. The symmetric-matrix cokernels of Chapter~\ref{ch:sandpiles} are the matrix theorem that matches one of these moments.
