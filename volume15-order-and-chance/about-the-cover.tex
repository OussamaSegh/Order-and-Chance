\chapter*{About the Cover}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Cover}
\noindent A histogram of the gaps between the first 1,200 zeros of the zeta function, unfolded so that the average gap is one, compared with the Gaudin–Mehta density of the Gaussian unitary ensemble and with the flat density of a Poisson process. Above it, a barcode of ninety consecutive zeros and a barcode of ninety Poisson points. Hugh Montgomery obtained the correlation in work he discussed with Freeman Dyson in 1972; Dyson recognised the ensemble. The paper was published in 1973. The chapter Random matrices and the zeros of zeta is this volume's telling of that meeting. The drawing was computed by the book's own TikZ cover generator.
