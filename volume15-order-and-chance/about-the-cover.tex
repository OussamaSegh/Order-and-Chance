\chapter*{About the Cover}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Cover}
\noindent The pair correlation of the first 1,200 zeros of the zeta function, unfolded so that the mean spacing is one. Every difference between two distinct zeros that lies between minus three and three is collected, and set against Montgomery's function, one minus the square of the quotient of sin(pi x) by pi x, the two-point correlation of the Gaussian unitary ensemble, and against the flat line of a Poisson process. Above the graph, a barcode of ninety-one consecutive zeros and a barcode of ninety Poisson points. Hugh Montgomery obtained the correlation in work he discussed with Freeman Dyson in 1972; Dyson recognised the ensemble. The paper was published in 1973. The chapter Random matrices and the zeros of zeta is this volume's telling of that meeting. The drawing was computed by the book's own TikZ cover generator.
