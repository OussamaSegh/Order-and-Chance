\chapter*{About the Cover}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Cover}
\noindent Two exact rows are joined by three vertical maps. Their kernels sit above them and their cokernels below. The long diagonal connects the last kernel to the first cokernel: it is the connecting homomorphism in the snake lemma. Chapter 3 constructs that map by lifting an element, following it down the diagram and taking a quotient class. The cover draws this diagram with the series cover generator. Its shape records a proof that will be used again in homological algebra.
