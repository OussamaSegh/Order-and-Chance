\chapter*{About the Cover}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Cover}
\noindent Two exact rows, three vertical maps, kernels along the top and cokernels along the bottom, and one long diagonal from the last kernel to the first cokernel. That diagonal is the connecting homomorphism of the snake lemma. Henri Cartan and Samuel Eilenberg set the lemma down in this form in Homological Algebra (1956), and it has been the first diagram chase of the subject ever since. This volume proves it in the chapter Tensors and Exactness, and uses it again when homological algebra begins in earnest. The drawing was computed by the book's own TikZ cover generator.
