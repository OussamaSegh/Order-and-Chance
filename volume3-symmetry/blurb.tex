Rotate a square, shuffle four cards, or exchange two roots of a polynomial. What stays the same? In this volume, that question leads from groups and quotients to fields and Galois theory. You will count necklaces without counting their rotations twice, see how a finite field can repair a damaged message, and find why no root of $X^5-4X+2$ can be obtained from rational numbers by radicals. Then we turn groups into matrices: the character table of the cube's rotation group explains the identity $24=1^2+1^2+2^2+3^2+3^2$. Quaternions bring the story back to rotations you can picture. The proofs grow from small examples, with graded exercises, hints and complete solutions.
