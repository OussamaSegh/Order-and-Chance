\chapter*{About the Cover}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Cover}
\noindent Thirteen points, the residues modulo 13, sit equally spaced on a circle. A chord joins two residues when their difference is a nonzero square modulo 13, one of 1, 3, 4, 9, 10 or 12. Minus 1 is a square modulo 13, so each chord is undirected. The graph has thirteen vertices, each of degree six, and thirty-nine edges. Adjacent vertices have two common neighbours, and vertices that are not adjacent have three. The three line weights mark the absolute differences 1, 3 and 4: heaviest for 1 and 12, middle for 3 and 10, lightest for 4 and 9. This is the Paley graph in the section Cayley graphs and the Paley graphs of the chapter Symmetry Diagonalises, the Cayley graph of addition modulo 13 on its nonzero squares.
