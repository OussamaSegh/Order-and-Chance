\chapter*{About the Cover}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Cover}
\noindent The cover draws the Collatz tree. Each integer from 2 up to 8,000 is joined to the next step of the map that sends an even number to its half and an odd number to one more than its triple, and the branches are those steps gathered back to 1. A pale curve marks the orbit of 27. Lothar Collatz posed the problem in 1937: does every positive integer reach 1? It is still open. The question is elementary and the proof is missing, which is the situation the opening chapter, The Language of Mathematics, asks the reader to recognise.
