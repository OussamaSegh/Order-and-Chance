\chapter*{Preface}
Tome~1 is about the integers: divisibility, primes, congruences, and the first steps of algebraic number theory.
Its centrepiece is the law of quadratic reciprocity, which Gauss called the \emph{theorema aureum}, and its
practical side is modern cryptography, which runs on the arithmetic of this volume.

It assumes Tome~0 (references such as ``Tome~0, Exercise~1.6'' point there). Exercises are graded
\textbf{$\star$} warm-up, \textbf{$\star\star$} oral-exam level, \textbf{$\star\star\star$} gems, and every exercise has a
complete solution at the end of its chapter. Each chapter has a companion Python notebook.
