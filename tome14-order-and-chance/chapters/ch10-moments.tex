\chapter{Moments of \texorpdfstring{$L$}{L}-functions}\label{ch:oc-moments}

This chapter states the Keating--Snaith conjecture for the moments of $\zeta$ on the critical line, and the recipe of Conrey, Farmer, Keating, Rubinstein and Snaith that extends it to families.

\section{Keating--Snaith}

Keating and Snaith proposed that
\[
\frac1T\int_0^T\Bigl|\zeta\Bigl(\tfrac12+it\Bigr)\Bigr|^{2k}\dd t
\]
has size $a_k\frac{G(k+1)^2}{G(2k+1)}(\log T)^{k^2}$,
where $a_k$ is an Euler product and $G$ is the Barnes function. The factor in $G$ is the moment of a characteristic polynomial of a random unitary matrix.

\section{The CFKRS recipe}

The recipe of Conrey, Farmer, Keating, Rubinstein and Snaith writes a conjectural asymptotic for moments in a family of $L$-functions by multiplying an arithmetic factor by the corresponding random-matrix moment. The chapter records the recipe; it does not prove it.
