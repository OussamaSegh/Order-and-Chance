\chapter{Random groups in arithmetic}\label{ch:oc-classgroups}

This chapter states the Cohen--Lenstra heuristics for class groups, as a probability measure on finite abelian groups weighted by the reciprocal of the number of automorphisms.

\section{The Cohen--Lenstra measure}

For odd primes $p$, the heuristic assigns to a finite abelian $p$-group $G$ a probability proportional to $1/|\operatorname{Aut} G|$. Equivalently, the moments are $\mathbb E\,\#\operatorname{Sur}(X,G)=1$.

\section{Class groups}

The proposal is that the $p$-part of the class group of an imaginary quadratic field, for $p$ odd, follows this measure. Davenport and Heilbronn proved the case of elements of order $3$: their average number is $1$. For $p\ge5$ the corresponding average is open.
