\chapter{Ranks of elliptic curves}\label{ch:oc-ranks}

This chapter collects the conjectures and theorems that predict the average rank of an elliptic curve, and the random-matrix model of Poonen and Rains for Selmer groups.

\section{Goldfeld's conjecture}

Goldfeld's conjecture says that, ordered by discriminant, half of the quadratic twists of an elliptic curve have rank $0$ and half have rank $1$, and that the average rank is $1/2$.

\section{Bhargava--Shankar averages}

Bhargava and Shankar proved that the average rank of elliptic curves over $\mathbb Q$, ordered by height, is at most $7/6$, by bounding the average size of a Selmer group.

\section{The Poonen--Rains model}

Poonen and Rains model a $p$-Selmer group by the intersection of two random maximal isotropic subspaces of an infinite-dimensional quadratic space over $\mathbb F_p$. The model predicts that the average size of the $p$-Selmer group is $p+1$.
