# Volume 4 — Chance

**Score: 6/10.**

The counting, discrete laws, generating functions, inequalities, entropy, Markov chains, and the graph and extremal arguments were read as proofs and hold, including the numerical checks named below. Three arguments do not. The sample standard deviation is compared with a difference of monotone clips, while the square is their sum. The strong-Markov tail says a stopped position is bounded by the hitting level. The fourth-moment strong law then applies Markov to a remainder that is not a finite-bit event.

## Read as holding

Ballot and the Catalan count. Boy–girl, including \(13/27\). Monty Hall. Penney. Birthdays, including \(365\ln 2<253\). Poisson approximation at \(n=100\), \(p=0.02\), and the bars for \(\mathrm{Bin}(20,1/10)\) and \(\mathrm{Poisson}(2)\). Cycles, coupons, and the long-cycle probability \(H_{2n}-H_n\to\ln 2\). Generating functions, branching-process extinction, and the Catalan generating function. Hoeffding, Kolmogorov, Borel–Cantelli, the bounded strong law on the digit model, and Bernstein. Gaussian tails, Box–Muller, and the stick-breaking probabilities once the slips above are set aside. The classical CLT, the local de Moivre–Laplace theorem, and the hundred-dice arithmetic. Entropy, Shannon, Huffman, and the typical set. Reflection for simple random walk, gambler’s ruin, and Pólya. Finite irreducible aperiodic chains, including the Bayer–Diaconis distances. Poisson processes, thinning, and M/M/1. Optional stopping, Azuma, and the ABRACADABRA mean. Dyadic quadratic variation. Erdős’s Ramsey bound, alteration, the local lemma, Euler’s formula apart from the degree-\(0\) sentence, and \(W(3,2)=9\).
