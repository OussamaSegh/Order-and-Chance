## All eight exercise/hint/solution contracts

1. `ex:thinning` — Poisson(λ) binomial thinning has PGF `exp(λp(s−1))`; includes p=0 and p=1.
2. `ex:golden` — f(1/2)=1/2 for p=2/3; m=4/3.
3. `ex:lotka` — masses 1/4,1/2,1/4 yield f=(1+s)^2/4, critical m=1, unique root 1.
4. `ex:randomsumvar` — conditioning yields `E[S]=E[N]μ`, `Var(S)=E[N]σ²+Var(N)μ²`; second-moment assumptions make each term finite. Checked with N=0,1,2 and Bernoulli summands.
5. `ex:progeny` — `h(s)=s f(h(s))` for finite total trees with defective generating function `h(1)=P(T<∞)`; empty child product is 1. Extinction need not have probability 1.
6. `ex:firstpassage` — first +1 or first −1 followed by two climbs: `F=ps+qsF²`. Independent increments after an `a.s.` finite first climb were justified directly by splitting into finite stopping records; no later Markov-chain theorem used. The small root gives `P(τ<∞)=min(1,p/q)`. Independently checked p=1/4,1/2,3/4 with exact fractions.
7. `ex:binseries` — `a_n/a_(n−1)=2(2n−1)/n`; termwise derivative of the central-binomial series justified by dominated difference quotients for |x|<1/4, not an unproved general power-series theorem. Differential equation `(1−4x)g'=2g`; Catalan coefficient derivative and generalized binomial coefficient formula checked.
8. `ex:poissongw` — for c>1 unique positive solution `1−ρ=e^(−cρ)` by convexity and sign; as c↓1 the nonzero root tends to 0 and the uniform second-order Taylor expansion gives `ρ~2(c−1)`. Numerical independent bisection for c=1.01,1.02,1.05,1.1 gives ratios ρ/(c−1) ≈1.97364,1.94788,1.87404,1.76134 approaching 2.
