## Mathematical scope and proofs retained

The chapter constructs Brownian paths by dyadic midpoint normals, proves independent Gaussian increments via Gaussian diagonal covariance, controls uniform convergence using normal tails and Borel–Cantelli, then extends by independent unit intervals. It proves scaling and time inversion including continuity at zero, quadratic variation along dyadic partitions with final short interval at arbitrary fixed $t$, infinite total variation, a restricted dyadic Taylor expansion (not a full Itô integral), nowhere finite differentiability, finite-observation martingale identity, restart at hitting times and reflection. Seven graded exercises, hints and worked solutions remain, including the zero-set proof by dyadic coverings rather than silently invoking Tonelli. No later Volume 12 theorem is used as a premise.
