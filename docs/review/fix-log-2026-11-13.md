### Why Appendix I was not moved in front of Chapter 9

The report offered two repairs: prove the distance and the angle defect in Chapter 9, or print Appendix I before that chapter. The first was taken.

Appendix I's proof that an h-line can be sent to the imaginary axis cited the Möbius theorem of Appendix B, which is printed after Chapter 9. Moving only Appendix I would have made that citation a new forward reference inside the proof the chapter needs. The same citation appeared in the disk model. Both appeals are now direct computations in Chapter 9: the real and imaginary parts of \((z-a)\overline{(b-z)}\) on the half-circle, and the image of the imaginary axis under the Cayley map together with the rotations of the disk. Chapter 9 does not cite a later appendix for a premise. Appendix I still tells the history of the parallel postulate, proves Girard's theorem, and keeps its exercises, which point back at the theorems in Chapter 9.

The cross-volume checker (`python3 tools/cross_volume_citations.py`) reports that the citations match the counters after this move. No later volume cited the old Appendix I numbers of the moved theorems.
