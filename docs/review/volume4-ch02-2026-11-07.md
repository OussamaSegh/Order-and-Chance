## Exercises, hints and complete solutions

All eight exercise environments, hint environments and solution environments occur once each and in exactly matching order:

1. **2.1 Records, `ex:records`:** uniform relative order puts the largest of first `k` values last with probability `1/k`; linearity yields `H_n`. Checked exact permutation enumeration through `n=7`.
2. **2.2 Fixed-point variance, `ex:fixedvar`:** off-diagonal covariance `1/[n²(n-1)]`; sum off-diagonals `1/n` and diagonal variances `1-1/n`, total 1 for `n>=2`. Exhaustive permutation checks through `n=7` (not valid at `n=1`, which the exercise excludes).
3. **2.3 Quicksort, `ex:quicksort`:** fresh uniform pivot assumption made explicit. The recurrence `a_n=n-1+(2/n)Σ_{j=0}^{n-1}a_j` telescopes to `2(n+1)H_n-4n`. Recurrence computed independently for `n=1..11`; output agrees with the closed form. The solution does not require subcalls to be independent for expected comparison counts.
4. **2.4 Poisson factorial moments, `ex:factorial`:** shift `j=k-m` in a nonnegative absolutely convergent exponential series; mean `λ`, variance `λ`. Checked `m=1,2` explicitly.
5. **2.5 Reservoir sampling, `ex:reservoir`:** probability an earlier input position survives `(1/(n-1))*(n-1)/n=1/n`, new position probability `1/n`; induction and repeated item values valid. Boundary `n=1` is trivial.
6. **2.6 Long cycles and prisoners, `ex:prisoners`:** for `k>m/2`, at most one such cycle; exactly `m!/k` permutations contain it; `P(X_k>=1)=1/k`. The distinct long-length events are disjoint for `k=n+1..2n`, giving **`H_{2n}-H_n -> ln 2`** by the written integral squeeze. Exhaustive permutations `m=2,4,6` confirm the formula. For 100 boxes, permutation `π(j)` is the label found in box `j`; following from box `i` encounters `i` on the last step of its cycle. Success `1-(H_100-H_50)=0.3118278206898…`. Wording revised to explain box-label and cycle mapping. Downstream Exercise 2.6 slot preserved.
7. **2.7 Birthday wait, `ex:birthdaywait`:** product tail and identity `E[W_N]=Σ_{m>=0}P(W_N>m)`, upper/lower exponential bounds for `m<=A√N`, Riemann-sum convergence, Gaussian-integral tail domination. Numeric normalized means for `N=100,1000,10000` are approximately `1.3210,1.2745,1.2600`, approaching `√(π/2)=1.253314…`. The three-star exercise legitimately uses the Gaussian integral actually proved in Volume 1.
8. **2.8 Two Poisson sums, `ex:twopoisson`:** independence yields a finite convolution for each `k`, the binomial theorem gives `e^{-(λ+μ)}(λ+μ)^k/k!`. Checked `k=0,1,2` directly.
