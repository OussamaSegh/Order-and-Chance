**V6-008, the constant `B`.** On the reviewed head the value `B=½log(4π)-1-γ/2` was computed before the residue in Chapter 7, from `ξ'/ξ(1)` and Legendre's duplication. The written step attributed the logarithm of the duplication formula to the theorem. The theorem states the product `Γ(x)Γ(x+½)=2^{1-2x}√π Γ(2x)`. The corrected paragraph takes logarithms, using that every factor is positive, and writes the substitution at `x=½` before concluding `ψ_Γ(½)=ψ_Γ(1)-2log 2`. The value at `s=1` is the continuous extension of the logarithmic derivative after `1/(s-1)+ζ'/ζ` has been replaced by `Z'/Z`. `Γ(½)=√π` is Volume 5, Proposition 5.12. **V6-009, the second proof of `thm:eta`.** Volume 5, Corollary 4.5 is `cor:dct`, dominated convergence for continuous functions. The cosine and sine formulas are in Volume 5, Chapter 9. Both signs of the imaginary part are needed, and the bound for `v>0` was already the right estimate of `|cot w+i|`. The sentence for `v<0` told the reader to multiply the numerator and denominator of the displayed formula for `cot w+i`. That produces `cot w+i`, not `cot w-i`. The corrected line writes `cot w-i=2i/(e^{2iw}-1)=2ie^{-2iw}/(1-e^{-2iw})` and then the bound `2e^{2v}/(1-e^{2v})`. The identity `cosh^2 v-sinh^2 v=1` is the expansion of the definitions, and `|sin w|^2` is the squared modulus divided by `|2i|^2=4`. On a compact subedge both imaginary parts are at least a positive multiple of `N`, so the convergence is uniform, and Corollary 4.5 applies to the real and imaginary parts on that finite interval. The corners contribute `O(η)` independently of `N`, and `η` is sent to `0` after `N`. The opening sentence that the proof needs only the residue theorem is no longer true, and it now names the exponential formulas and the corollary. The identity `(*)` and the transformation of `η` are unchanged. The product of the two cotangent limits is `+1` on `[1,τ]` and `[-1,-τ]`, and `-1` on the other two open edges.
