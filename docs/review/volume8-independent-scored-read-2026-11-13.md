### Overall

**7.**

The main path is a real analysis book. The compact spectral theorem, Cheeger’s inequality, Meshulam’s argument, Ikehara’s theorem and the \(p\)-adic Fourier transform are proved, with hypotheses, and the numerical claims that were recomputed come out right. I would not hand it to a reader as it stands. Chapter 16 still sends the reader to a Problem Book this series does not contain, Chapter 10 still says “Chapter 19,” there is no index, and the KDP text calls the Dirac delta a function. Those are repairs, not a rewrite. After them, and after the angular-convention citations in Volumes 11 and 14 are pointed at the change of variables this book already writes down, I would recommend it to the reader who has finished Volume 7.

## Verified correct

These were read and, where a number appears, recomputed. A numerical check is not the proof; the proof was also read.

- Hahn–Banach analytic and geometric forms, Riesz’s lemma, and the open-mapping constant chase (`ch01-banach.tex:92`, `ch01-banach.tex:196`).
- Lebesgue constants to three decimals, and the lower bound \((4/\pi^2)\sum_{k=1}^N 1/k\).
- The second Radon–Nikodym proof and \(\int_{-1}^1 P_n^2=2/(2n+1)\) (`ch02-hilbert.tex:237`, `ch02-hilbert.tex:165`).
- The normal spectral theorem over \(\mathbb C\), and the real symmetric case (`linear-ch05-spectral.tex:55`).
- SVD of a general rectangular matrix; singular values \(3\sqrt5\) and \(\sqrt5\) for the printed ellipse; Eckart–Young in the operator norm and the Frobenius norm.
- Friendship, the Hoffman–Singleton list \(\{2,3,7,57\}\), Hoffman’s bound, Graham–Pollak, and \(A_n^2=nI\) in Huang’s proof. Petersen spectrum \(3,1^5,-2^4\).
- Cheeger \(\mu_2/2\le h\le\sqrt{2d\mu_2}\), the mixing lemma, and the Alon–Boppana test-vector computation. The cycle solution \(h(C_n)=2/\lfloor n/2\rfloor\).
- Sunada’s equivalence, and \(|\pi(k)-q^k/k|\le 6n q^{k/2}/k\) for a non-bipartite Ramanujan graph.
- Fredholm’s alternative for compact operators on Hilbert space, Gelfand’s spectral-radius formula, and \(r(V)=0\) for the Volterra operator with no eigenvalues.
- The compact self-adjoint spectral theorem, Courant–Fischer for \(K\ge 0\), Sturm–Liouville bounds \(\pi^2 n^2\le\lambda_n\le\pi^2 n^2+\max q\), the four eigenvalues for \(q=60x\), Mercer’s trace, \(\min(s,t)\) modes, \(\|V\|=2/\pi\), and \(\sum n^{-2}=\pi^2/6\), \(\sum n^{-4}=\pi^4/90\) from the string.
- Fourier inversion and Plancherel in the unitary convention, \(\mathcal F^4=I\) on the Schwartz class, the three-lines lemma, and the Cauchy characteristic function \(e^{-a|t|}\).
- \(H'=\delta\), Sobolev embedding for \(s>1/2\), the factor \(1/(4\pi^2)\) in the \(H^1\) norm, Rellich on an interval, Lax–Milgram.
- Quadratic reciprocity from the Chinese-remainder factorisation of the Gauss sum, using Volume 5’s sign. Meshulam’s \(32\cdot 3^n/n\). BLR.
- Abelian uniqueness of Haar measure, the \(GL_n\) density \(|\det X|^{-n}\), and \(\mu_p(a+p^k\mathbb Z_p)=p^{-k}\).
- Phragmén–Lindelöf in a half-strip, the convexity bound \(O_\varepsilon(|t|^{1/4+\varepsilon})\), Hadamard’s genus \(\lfloor\rho\rfloor\), and Mellin inversion as Fourier inversion after \(x=e^u\).
- Wiener’s Tauberian theorem under \(\hat f\ne 0\), and Ikehara’s theorem as printed. The input “no zero on \(\operatorname{Re}s=1\)” is Volume 6, Theorem 5.2.

- The Hadamard-matrix status in Appendix E, against arXiv:2610.27175.
- Exercise coverage: 212 exercises, 212 hints, 212 solutions, and all 46 `\fig` files exist.
