The think-box answer asks for \(2n-1\) roots of unity, because a product of two polynomials of degree \(n-1\) has degree at most \(2n-2\). Any larger order works as well.

In Corollary `cor:polymul`, a negative unwrapped index \(k-l\) is stored as \(k-l+n\). For a nonzero padded factor one has \(l\le d\), so that stored index is at least \(n-d>e\), and the padded coefficient is zero.
