3. Compile the actual native book, inspect high-resolution figure placement and every affected page; verify photo source, credit, licence and what the photo truly depicts.
4. Build/validate EPUB, online reader, reading PDF, KDP print interior and paperback wrap; record actual checks and artefact hashes. The supplemental `.github/workflows/volume4-qa.yml` is not a substitute for the original protected full CI workflow.
