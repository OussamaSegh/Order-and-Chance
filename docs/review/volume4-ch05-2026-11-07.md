## Nine graded exercises / nine hints / nine full solutions

All labels and original exercise order retained, with complete solution `\idea` and `\writeup` on each:

1. `ex:counter`: tent height n base 2/n area 1; at each nonzero x eventually 0, at 0 diverges. For n=2^m disjoint intervals [1/(2n),1/n) each have tent area 1/8, proving no common integrable nonnegative dominant; DCT hypotheses fail.
2. `ex:densityunique`: equal interval integrals imply H(x)=∫_0^x(f−g)≡0 and H′=f−g=0 by Volume 1 FTC.
3. `ex:tail`: Mills-bound proof as `prop:gausstail`, then divide by √(2π) for standard normal probability. At x=1, actual tail 0.1586552539≤bound 0.2419707245.
4. `ex:cauchy`: ∫ dx/(π(1+x²))=1 from arctangent; |x|/(1+x²)≥1/(2|x|) for |x|≥1, divergent logarithmic absolute first moment.
5. `ex:gamma`: I_1=1, I_(n+1)=n I_n; exact independent numerical integrals n=1..5 equal 1,1,2,6,24 factorial values.
6. `ex:order`: kth of n independent uniforms has density n*binom(n−1,k−1)t^(k−1)(1−t)^(n−k), beta integral by elementary repeated parts; mean k/(n+1), checked by independent quadrature at (n,k)=(1,1),(2,1),(2,2),(5,1),(5,3),(8,8), normalization 1.
7. `ex:cantor`: middle-third survivors total length (2/3)^n; binary-digit ternary series remains in survivor set for every outcome, every individual value has probability 0, CDF continuous. Density continuous except finitely many points would have to vanish on dense union of removed open thirds and outside [0,1], so integrate to zero, contradiction; stronger result now explained.
8. `ex:usquare`: U² has CDF √y and density 1/(2√y) on (0,1), with integrable singularity at 0; mean 1/3 matches E[U²].
9. `ex:radius`: Box–Muller R²=−2lnU has exponential(1/2) survival e^(−t/2), density (1/2)e^(−t/2), t>0. `P(R>1)=e^(−1/2)` checked.
