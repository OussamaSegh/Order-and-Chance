# Volume 5 round-1 binary-digit repair, second reading

Mathematics confirmed in the working tree that this note accompanies. This note does not change the mathematics. No LaTeX build was run for this reading.

Blocks are sorted by the exponent, not by the index of the bit. For \(N=2^k+1\) the second exponent is \(0\). Small blocks, those with exponent below \(k-h\), have total weight less than \(2^{-h}\). A large block has shift at most \(h\), and the passage from the fair-binomial central limit theorem to the normalised count is a \(\delta/\eta\) sandwich. The limit is \(N\to\infty\), then \(\eta\downarrow 0\), then \(h\to\infty\).

The citation is the classical central limit theorem in Volume 4, Chapter 6, not the local point-mass theorem.
