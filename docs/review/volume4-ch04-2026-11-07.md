## Six exercises, hints and complete solutions

All labels and slots preserved, with exactly one matching hint and full `\idea`/`\writeup` solution for each.

1. `ex:markovsharp`: for fixed a>0 and E[X]=a, 0<p≤1 gives X=a/p with probability p and zero otherwise. No p>1 possible. Equality in Markov with nonconstant 0/a variable uses a *different* mean qa, not a.
2. `ex:chernoffbin`: optimize `λa−ln(1−p+pe^λ)`, λ>0 at `e^λ=a(1−p)/(p(1−a))`. Second derivative strictly negative for 0<p<a<1. Bound exp(−nD(a||p)); p=1/2 check.
3. `ex:bcfinite`: stage n≥2 with radix n gives independent digit event mass 1/n and divergent harmonic sum; radix n² gives mass 1/n² and convergent sum. Stage n=1 is the deterministic full-space event, not an illegal radix-two-plus digit; solution explicitly clarified at `9c9ae12e`.
4. `ex:bernstein2`: `B_n(x²)=x²+x(1−x)/n` by binomial mean and variance, `B_2=(x+x²)/2` and uniform error ≤1/(4n).
5. `ex:gapfill`: only using Chebyshev n_k=k gives bound ∑1/k divergent (does *not* mean the actual event probabilities must diverge). Using `ceil(k^(1+δ))`, `1/n_k≤k^(−1−δ)` summable.
6. `ex:runs`: upper tail window length `floor((1+ε)log_2 n)`, at most n windows each probability ≤2n^(−1−ε); lower tail disjoint blocks of `ceil((1−ε)log_2 n)`, independent failures and `floor(n/s)≥n/(2s)` for large n. Explicit `n^ε/(2((1−ε)log_2n+1))→∞` justifies the product →0. Ceiling/floor inclusion checked for 0<ε<1. Limit is in probability only; no unsupported almost-sure claim.
