| thm:os | At bounded stopping time τ≤N, telescoping Xτ−X0=Σ_(k≤N)1_{τ≥k}(X_k−X_(k−1)). The factor 1_{τ≥k} is determined by the information at time k−1, so its conditional product expectation is zero. Sum finite, no invalid unbounded limit. | thm:os2 | (1) is bounded τ. For (2), finite Eτ implies τ finite and |Xτ−X_(τ∧n)|≤c(τ−n)_+; its expectation is ≤cΣ_(j>n)P(τ≥j)→0 via the tail-sum identity. | thm:upcross | Predictable switch H_k∈{0,1} turns on at X_(k−1)≤a and off at ≥b. Trading gain Y_n=ΣH_kΔX_k has mean zero. Each completed upcrossing contributes at least b−a, unfinished last gain ≥−(a−X_n)_+. Hence (b−a)U_n≤Y_n+(a−X_n)_+, expectation gives bound. The switch at previous rather than current level correctly includes an arrival at b on last increment. |
