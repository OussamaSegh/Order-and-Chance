- **C11-N09, critical casino accounting convention:** The text said a successful gambler stops, then used `W_t−t` as a martingale without specifying whether the completed prize is still counted. Added small self-overlap example: fair HT mean 4, HH mean 6 (also corroborated against the University of California Data 140 probability text). **Writer-corrected; external independent referee still pending.**

- **C11-N10, proof hypothesis gap — square-mean Cauchy (Prop 11.8):** Its statement assumes ∑ E[(X_k−X_{k-1})²]<∞ but not E[X_0²]<∞. The old proof invoked Prop 11.7, whose hypothesis is finite second moments of every fortune; this invocation was unjustified when X0 is integrable but not in L², even though the *difference* conclusion is valid. Source `bc58c7386796a65d26aff3f8e1a8a7db71073e71` gives a direct proof: each increment D_k is in L² by assumption, D_j for j<k is known at time k−1, so E[D_jD_k]=0 using conditional centring and Cauchy–Schwarz. Expanding the finite increment sum yields E[(X_n−X_m)²]=∑_{k=m+1}^n E[D_k²], whose tail tends to zero, uniformly in n>m. Clarified Prop 11.7 as requiring E[X_k²]<∞ for every k. Also explicitly verified Doob's terminal-square-integrability implies square integrability of every preceding X_k by conditional Jensen/tower. These fixes preserve the original theorem strengths and printed numbers. **Writer corrected and checked; a distinct independent reviewer is pending.**
