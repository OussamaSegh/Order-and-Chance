## Independent calculations on the actual manuscript

I revisited source passages across all four parts and Appendix B. Reading a theorem or inspecting its proof is not, alone, a mathematical signoff. The following finite calculations use separately written algorithms, not checks for particular source strings. The new test suite tools/test_volume4_phase2_independent_finite_exercises.py has nine unittest methods; all nine passed in local execution before GitHub CI.

1. Printed Chapters 1, 3: For a=1..8 and b=0..a-1, enumerate all ballot orders, verify every partial tally and compare the exact counts with (a-b)/(a+b). Exhaustively enumerate all 2^(2n) paths for n=1..7 and verify the Catalan count binom(2n,n)/(n+1). This checks thm:ballot, ex:smallballot and ex:catalan finite cases.
2. Printed Chapter 8: Enumerate all 2^(2n) fair walks for n=1..5. Compare last return and positive-edge occupation distributions and verify the finite arcsine identity u_(2k)u_(2n-2k) and probability of remaining nonnegative. This supports the finite thm:arcsine and ex:timepositive, but does not independently establish the arcsine-limit integral.
3. Printed Chapter 8: Independently solve exact rational first-step hitting/duration systems for absorbing gambler's ruin on N=2..8 with p=1/3,1/2,2/3. The answers match thm:ruin and prop:duration, including ex:unfair.
4. Printed Chapters 2 and 6: Exhaustively enumerate all permutations through n=7 (5,040 permutations at n=7). Verify that fixed-point mean and variance are both 1 for n>=2 and expected cycles H_n. Independently solve the quicksort expected-cost recurrence through n=25 and recover 2(n+1)H_n-4n.
5. Printed Chapter 2: From exact first-step recursions compute the first and second coupon-collector moments for n=1..10. Confirm mean nH_n and variance n^2 sum(1/k^2)-nH_n; this is independent of the stage-variance proof in the book.
6. Printed Chapter 7: Independently implement optimal Huffman merging on the 9 two-symbol block probabilities generated by 1/2,1/3,1/6. Total expected block length exactly 107/36, beating 3/2 per original symbol.
7. Printed Chapter 9: Exact rational three-state Markov row multiplication verifies stationary row (2/7,3/7,2/7), two-step first row (5/12,5/12,1/6), and total variation distance 11/84.
8. Printed Chapter 14: Exhaustively enumerate every labelled graph on n=3,4,5 (up to 1,024 graphs), count triangles and squared triangle counts, compare with formulas for independent p=1/2 edges and overlapping triangle pairs.
9. Printed Chapters 4, 13: Exact 1,000-coin tail mass above 599 heads lies strictly between 1.3642e-10 and 1.3643e-10; binom(27,7)=888030, binom(28,7)=1184040, binom(33,7)=4272048; Ramsey alteration n-E[X]=1895685/65536 and the K20 crossing calculation is 267+119/128.
