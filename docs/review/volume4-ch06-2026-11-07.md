## Eight exercise / hint / fully worked solution records

All eight existing graded exercise labels, all eight exact matching hint keys, all eight exact matching solutions with `\idea`/`\writeup` retained, in original order; no invented new questions.

1. `ex:slutsky`: bounded uniformly continuous tests control X_n+Y_n. Distribution sandwich at continuity points, tightness of X_n, negative-multiplier endpoint caveat, product in probability with vanishing error and CDF convergence. **No black-box Slutsky borrowed**.
2. `ex:confidence`: exact identity `hatσ_n²=n^(-1)ΣY_i²−barY_n²`; finite fourth moment ensures variance of Y², weak law and sample variance consistency. Expanded zero-sample-variance event `P(hatσ_n=0)→0`, the distributional limit of Studentised mean, and **asymptotic**, not finite-sample, coverage `2Φ(1.96)−1`.
3. `ex:delta`: local MVT and continuity of g′ at μ give error factor ε_n→0 in probability on the high-probability neighbourhood, with the exceptional event explicitly excluded from its equality. Tightness of CLT-scaled mean makes product error negligible, Slutsky completes.
4. `ex:cauchyclt`: original detailed Riemann damping computation yields characteristic function `exp(−|t|)`; product gives `exp(−√n|t|)`, discontinuous at 0 in limit, contradicting continuity of any limiting CF. Finite second moment fails; even E|Y|=∞.
5. `ex:poissonhalf`: Poisson(n) is sum of n independent Poisson(1); standardise via CLT, CDF at 0 tends `Φ(0)=1/2`.
6. `ex:returns`: independent Stirling central binomial and local theorem both give `P(S_(2m)=0)~1/sqrt(πm)`.
7. `ex:thirdmoment`: normal `E|Z|³=2sqrt(2/π)=1.5957691216057308`; smooth-test M=1,n=100 bound `(1+2sqrt(2/π))/60=0.043262818693428844`. Checked dimensionless scaling.
8. `ex:cyclesclt`: `E C_n=H_n`, `v_n=H_n−Σ1/k²=ln n+O(1)`. Centered summands individually bounded by `1/sqrt(v_n)`, making Lindeberg sum exactly 0 eventually. Smooth cutoff gives CDF, and Slutsky replaces H_n and v_n by ln n.
