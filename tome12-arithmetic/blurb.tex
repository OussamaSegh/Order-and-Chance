In the ring of numbers $a+b\sqrt{-5}$, the number $6$ factors in two different ways, and unique factorisation is lost.
This book follows the repair, by Kummer and Dedekind, and the world it opened. Prime numbers split into prime ideals
according to a single symmetry, the Frobenius. Minkowski's geometry of lattices proves that class groups are finite and
that units form a lattice. Kummer's cyclotomic fields give the first serious attack on Fermat's Last Theorem. The $p$-adic
numbers, the local--global principle and the ad\`eles look at all primes at once, and Tate's thesis derives the functional
equation of the zeta function from Fourier analysis. Class field theory describes every abelian extension of the rationals.
The last part turns to curves: the group of points of an elliptic curve, the Riemann hypothesis for curves over finite
fields, and modular forms. Every result is proved in full, except two clearly marked theorems of class field theory. Each
chapter opens with the story behind its ideas, ends with exercises and complete solutions, and comes with a Python
notebook.
