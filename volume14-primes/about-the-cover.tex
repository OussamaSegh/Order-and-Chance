\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Cover}
\noindent The path of the Riemann zeta function as one walks up the critical line, from height 10 to height 42, plotted as a curve in the plane. The curve passes through the origin at every zero. Bernhard Riemann's memoir of 1859, on the number of primes less than a given magnitude, placed those zeros at the centre of the theory of the primes. The chapter The Critical Line is where this volume studies them. The drawing was computed by the book's own TikZ cover generator.
