# Volume 5 portraits

Every photograph in this volume is stored here. Nothing is hotlinked. Captions in the chapters repeat the credit. Print height is capped in `main.tex` at 1.65 in, or at pixel-height/300 when that is shorter (`abel` 1.08 in, `cauchy` 1.29 in), so the short side stays at least 300 pixels per inch.

Seven files are byte-for-byte copies of the public-domain portraits already used in Volume 1. Their chapter captions there are the provenance. The hashes below are of the files in this directory.
