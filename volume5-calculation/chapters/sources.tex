\chapter*{Sources}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Sources}
\noindent Each chapter ends with the papers and books it uses, under Sources and further reading. The lines below point back at those lists. A result that is quoted rather than proved is marked at the place it is used.

\begin{description}
\item[Chapter~1, Finite Calculus] Graham, Knuth and Patashnik, \emph{Concrete Mathematics}; Knuth on Faulhaber; Bernoulli, \emph{Ars Conjectandi}; Boole and Jordan on finite differences; Ireland and Rosen; Conway and Guy; Swade on Babbage's engine.
\item[Chapter~2, Generating Functions] Wilf, \emph{generatingfunctionology}; Graham, Knuth and Patashnik; Flajolet and Sedgewick; Stanley; Andrews; Franklin's proof of the pentagonal theorem; Hardy and Wright; Knuth on quicksort; Euler's \emph{Introductio} and the 1751 paper on the sum of divisors.
\item[Chapter~3, Series and Products] Aigner and Ziegler, \emph{Proofs from THE BOOK}; Euler on reciprocal squares; Dunham; Remmert; Niven's proof that $\pi$ is irrational; Ritelli; Ap\'ery and van der Poorten; Borwein and Borwein; Perron; Berggren, Borwein and Borwein, \emph{Pi: A Source Book}.
\item[Chapter~4, Integration] Feynman; Nahin; Boros and Moll; Rudin; Arias de Reyna on Frullani; Bressoud; Bailey, Borwein and Plouffe (1997) for the hexadecimal series; D.~and J.~Borwein on the sinc integrals.
\item[Chapter~5, Gamma and Beta] Artin, \emph{The Gamma Function}; Bohr and Mollerup; Davis; Andrews, Askey and Roy; Remmert on Wielandt; Fulton and Harris; Nair; Dalzell and Lucas; Borwein, Bailey and Girgensohn; Lanczos.
\item[Chapter~6, Fourier Series] K\"orner; Stein and Shakarchi; Katznelson; Hurwitz's isoperimetric argument; Weyl (1916); Fourier; Fej\'er; Carleson; Kahane and Katznelson; Rudin on flat polynomials; Siegel's convex-body argument; Hewitt and Hewitt on the Gibbs phenomenon.
\item[Chapter~7, The DFT and the FFT] Cooley and Tukey; Heideman, Johnson and Burrus on Gauss; Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest and Stein; Knuth; Harvey and van der Hoeven; Wallace on JPEG; Strang; Karatsuba and Ofman; Sch\"onhage and Strassen; Chung, Diaconis and Graham; Newman; Rothaus; Donoho and Stark; Tao; Cooley on how the FFT was taken up.
\item[Chapter~8, The Fourier Transform] Stein and Shakarchi; Dym and McKean; Berndt and Evans on the Gauss sum; Dirichlet (1835); Whittaker; Shannon; Ireland and Rosen.
\item[Chapter~9, Complex Analysis] Stein and Shakarchi; Ahlfors; Needham; Goursat; Cauchy (1825); Remmert on Wielandt; Painlev\'e and Hadamard; Rademacher and Grosswald; Vardi; Jensen (1899); Mitrinovi\'c and Ke\v cki\'c.
\item[Chapter~10, The Zeta Function] Riemann (1859); Edwards; Apostol; Titchmarsh; Euler (E72); Hardy (1914); Selberg; Odlyzko; Gourdon; Casimir; Kuzmin; Wirsing; Tao on Euler--Maclaurin; Platt and Trudgian.
\item[Chapter~11, Asymptotics] Abel's letter to Holmboe; Euler (E47); Erd\H os and R\'enyi; de Bruijn; Graham, Knuth and Patashnik; Flajolet and Sedgewick; Flajolet and Odlyzko; Whittaker and Watson; Hardy, \emph{Divergent Series}; Newman.
\item[Chapter~12, The Hardy--Ramanujan Formula] Hardy and Ramanujan (1918); Rademacher (1937); Lehmer; Newman, \emph{Analytic Number Theory}, for the major and minor arcs. Rademacher's series is quoted in the chapter and is not proved here.
\item[Chapter~13, The Arithmetic--Geometric Mean] Borwein and Borwein, \emph{Pi and the AGM}; Cox; Newman; Salamin; Brent; Marchisotto and Zakeri; Gauss's diary; Legendre; the Chudnovskys; Serre; Granville.
\end{description}
