Some of the most beautiful results in mathematics are exact values: the sum of the inverse squares is $\pi^2/6$, the
Gaussian integral is $\sqrt\pi$, and in a precise sense $1+2+3+\cdots$ is $-1/12$. Others are approximations so good that
they are almost as striking, like Stirling's formula or Gauss's arithmetic--geometric mean, which doubles the number of
correct digits of $\pi$ at every step. This book is about the art of calculating: finding the exact value of a sum, a
product or an integral, and, when none exists, an approximation with an error one can bound. Its tools are finite
calculus and generating functions, the Gamma function, Fourier analysis on the circle, on finite groups, where it
becomes the fast Fourier transform, and on the line, complex analysis and residues, and the zeta function with its
functional equation. Every result is proved in full, with graded exercises and complete solutions.
