\chapter*{About the Cover}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Cover}
\noindent The cover draws partial sums of the Fourier series of a square wave. A paler line is the wave itself. Beneath the wave, bars mark the odd harmonics of its spectrum. Joseph Fourier published the series in The Analytical Theory of Heat in 1822. At each jump the sums overshoot, and that overshoot does not shrink when further terms are added. Henry Wilbraham noticed the overshoot in 1848. J. Willard Gibbs brought it back to attention in 1899. This volume takes the series up again in the chapter The Fourier Transform.
