\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Cover}
\noindent Partial sums of the Fourier series of a square wave, the wave itself in a paler line, and beneath them the odd harmonics of its spectrum. Joseph Fourier published the series in The Analytical Theory of Heat in 1822. The overshoot at each jump, which refuses to shrink as further terms are added, was noticed by Henry Wilbraham in 1848 and brought back to attention by J. Willard Gibbs in 1899. The chapter The Fourier Transform is where this volume takes up that analysis. The drawing was computed by the book's own TikZ cover generator.
