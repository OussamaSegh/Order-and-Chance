# Historical illustrations used in Volume 2

The three files below are the actual portraits included in the manuscript.
The Commons file records identify them as public-domain reproductions. Local
files and downloaded originals were viewed on4 November2026 and compared
numerically; they are resized/recompressed derivatives, not identical bytes.
Hashes, dimensions and comparison metrics are in
[the source evidence](../../docs/reviews/volume2-portrait-source-evidence-2026-11-04.json).

| File | Subject/source | Attribution and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fermat.jpg` | [Pierre de Fermat](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pierre_de_Fermat.jpg) | Traditional portrait, artist unknown. Commons assigns17th century but its permission note qualifies the original's authenticity. The reader caption avoids asserting a precise date or the disputed1601 birth year. |
| `gauss.jpg` | [Carl Friedrich Gauss1840 by Jensen](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carl_Friedrich_Gauss_1840_by_Jensen.jpg) | Christian Albrecht Jensen,1840. This is the visually/numerically matching source, distinct from the Commons file named Carl Friedrich Gauss.jpg, whose description credits an1887 Biermann copy. |
| `chebyshev.jpg` | [Pafnuty Lvovich Chebyshev](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pafnuty_Lvovich_Chebyshev.jpg) | Photographer unknown,late1880s or early1890s. Commons cites History of the USSR Academy of Sciences,Nauka1964,volume2,pp296–297. Original print/photographer not independently located. |

`minkowski.jpg` remains an unused repository asset; it is not included in the
book or its reader-facing credits. This replaces the obsolete four-portrait/
Volume0 provenance statement. Original mathematical diagrams are maintained
as TikZ sources in `../figures/`. This record documents source/attribution
checks and does not claim independent publisher/legal approval.
