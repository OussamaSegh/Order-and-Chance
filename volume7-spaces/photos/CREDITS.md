# Volume 7 portraits

Every photograph in this volume is stored here. Nothing is hotlinked. Each chapter caption repeats the credit. Print height is the series cap of 1.65 in, or pixel-height/300 when that is shorter, so the short side stays at least 300 pixels per inch. The shorter caps are set in `main.tex`: Baire 1.343 in, Dedekind, Fréchet, Fubini and Jordan 1.087 in, Kolmogorov 1.110 in, Urysohn 0.837 in, Vitali 1.433 in.

Files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 13 November 2026. GIF and PNG originals were saved as JPEG. No scene was edited. Hashes are of the files in this directory.

The share-alike term applies to Kolmogorov’s photograph, not to the text of the book. Banach, Nikodym, Khinchin and Riesz are not here: the files found on Commons were a monument, a tomb, or a portrait whose public-domain claim had no source. No portrait was used when the only available file had an unclear right.

The Borel autochrome is kept in colour. The orange speckles are on the 1918 plate. They were not added, and the file was not converted to grey.
