\emph{Order and Chance} is a series of books of beautiful mathematics, written for the pleasure
of understanding. It travels from the integers, symmetry and chance to the questions studied by
mathematicians today, where number theory, algebra and probability meet.\par\medskip
Every theorem is proved in full. Every chapter has exercises chosen for their beauty, each with
a hint and a complete solution that explains how one finds the idea before writing it down, and
a companion Python notebook in which the mathematics is tested, pictured and put to work.
