\chapter{Moments of \texorpdfstring{$L$}{L}-functions}\label{ch:oc-moments}\label{ch:moments}

The Keating--Snaith conjecture and the recipe that turns a random-matrix moment into a prediction for $\zeta$. What is proved here is the mean square, with Ingham's secondary term, the lower bounds of the conjectured order, and the arithmetic factor $a_k$ read from the random Euler product of Section~\ref{ch:bohrjessen}. Moments of $\zeta$ on the critical line past the fourth, which is quoted, stay conjectural. The matrix moments are Volume~11, Chapter~11.
