\chapter{When order wins}\label{ch:oc-bias}\label{ch:bias}

Chebyshev's bias is proved here, under the generalised Riemann hypothesis and linear independence of the zeros, as a computation of a logarithmic density. Maier's irregularities are Chapter~\ref{ch:gaps}. The bias of Lemke Oliver and Soundararajan for consecutive primes is stated, not proved.
