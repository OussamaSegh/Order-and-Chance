\chapter{Order and chance in the integers}\label{ch:oc-integers}\label{ch:anatomy}

A deterministic set of integers can match the mean and the variance of a random model and still fail a short-interval test. This chapter fixes what ``random-like'' will mean, through the anatomy of a typical integer: normal order, friable integers, and the Dickman function. Cram\'er's model is defined in Chapter~\ref{ch:oc-models}; the distribution of $\omega(n)$ is proved in Chapter~\ref{ch:oc-divisors}.
