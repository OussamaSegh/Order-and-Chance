Every journey in mathematics starts with the same tools, and this book sharpens them until they are a pleasure to use.
It covers sums and induction, complex numbers and the roots of unity, polynomials, inequalities, limits and series,
integrals and Taylor's formulas, linear algebra, and counting with the first steps in probability. Each tool comes with
the problems that make it shine: roots of unity that turn a sum of binomial coefficients into a picture, one inequality
between means that settles a dozen optimisation problems, a determinant that measures how a map stretches volume.
Every statement is proved, and the few foundations assumed are named and proved later in the series. Most of the pages
are exercises, graded from warm-ups to gems, each with a hint and a complete solution that first explains how to find the
idea and only then writes it down. Pause-and-think boxes ask you to guess before you read on, and every chapter has a
companion Python notebook where the mathematics is tested and drawn.
