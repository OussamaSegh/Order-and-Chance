A probability is a model you write down before you compute. This book builds one sample space that holds an infinite sequence of tosses, starting from the finite experiments of Volume~1. You will meet the ballot theorem, Hoeffding's inequality, the central limit theorem, P\'olya's theorem on random walks, and the probabilistic method. The Gaussian integral becomes the law of a sum of many small independent pieces. Every step used in a proof is proved in this volume or in Volume~1. Each chapter has graded exercises with complete solutions.
