Is there always a prime between $x$ and $x+\sqrt x$? Are there infinitely many pairs of primes at bounded distance? This book takes the reader from the prime number theorem to the research frontier of analytic and additive number theory, with complete proofs: zero-density estimates and primes in short intervals, Siegel's theorem,
sieves and the parity problem, the Bombieri--Vinogradov theorem, Vinogradov's three primes theorem, the Hardy--Ramanujan formula for partitions, Maynard's bounded gaps between primes, Hal\'asz's theorem, Roth's theorem on progressions, Gowers norms and the Green--Tao theorem, Hardy's theorem on the critical line and Montgomery's pair correlation of the zeros of the zeta function. Each chapter opens with the
story behind its ideas, ends with exercises and complete solutions, and comes with a Python notebook.
