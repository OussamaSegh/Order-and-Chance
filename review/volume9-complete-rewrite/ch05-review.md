## Mathematical corrections and prerequisites

- Equal-time reparametrization of finite concatenations is explained before subdivision in van Kampen. An empty factorization is represented by a constant loop. Only the homotopy square's left/right edges and the bottom/top factor joins are necessarily at the basepoint. Connector paths are chosen in all incident rectangle and boundary-factor sets. The full grid swap argument gives both kernel inclusions.
- A local bouquet computation is supplied before surface presentations, removing their dependence on the later graph theorem. The sphere/projective-space computation before van Kampen is explicitly a preview. Genus zero and RP0/RP1 exceptions are retained explicitly.
- Power coverings require nonzero exponent and have absolute-exponent sheet count. The empty-sheet convention is retained on missing components rather than silently narrowing the definition. Fibre cardinality is distinguished from globally constant sheet count on a disconnected base.
- Winding classifies fixed-endpoint based loops with the SAME basepoint. Homotopy-inverse induced maps track their different basepoints. Covering classification supplies good-neighbourhood basepoint transfer, topology basis, subgroup, path-connectedness and unique isomorphism; universal factoring is explained locally.
- The deck criterion requires BOTH subgroup inclusions for a homeomorphism, not just the inclusion sufficient for a lift. Right-coset and deck composition conventions are kept consistent.
- Free-product associativity is proved by permutation operators, including the cancellation case and identity/empty factors. Normal closure and free rank zero are defined.
- The finite-graph and locally finite infinite-graph arguments are complete, with finite compact-image control. The arbitrary-rank extension is separated: star-cover reductions of loops and nullhomotopies repair the invalid blanket claim that compact images always lie in finite subgraphs. Zorn's lemma is available in earlier Volume 7, Theorem 1.19. Infinite index is explicitly NOT used as a reason for infinite rank; <a> in F2 is a counterexample.
- The old blanket assertion that the fundamental group of a surface is not free is removed (the sphere already contradicts it). The surface-cover Euler formula is explicitly a finite-cover preview of Chapter 6, never a premise here.
- The polynomial solution keeps its normalized basepoint fixed throughout its large-circle homotopy. The third two-sheeted-cover basis change is proved invertible. Grid commutator rank follows from its actual infinite basis, and commutator normality is justified.
- The two-nail solution supplies an explicit strong deformation retraction using touching diamonds and vertical projection, rather than merely asserting a spine. The three-puncture free group is justified by van Kampen. Projective maps lift from an appropriate chosen initial point.
