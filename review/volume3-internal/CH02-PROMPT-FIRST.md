- **ex:autzn:** image of [1] fixes f([k])=k a. Every a works, and automorphism iff
  a is a unit (preimage of1 supplies inverse). Composition multiplies coefficients.
  n1 is the one-element case, including its sole unit.
- **ex:centre:** Z={z:zg=gz for every g}; it is closed under products/inverses and
  conjugation fixes each z. If G/Z=<gZ>, write x=g^i z,y=g^j w; xy=g^(i+j)zw=yx.
  Finite or infinite quotient works.
- **ex:phimult:** the explicit reduction map used in group CRT also preserves
  multiplication and1. Units correspond both ways; invert the pair of residues,
  lift it and use injectivity to check a times its lifted inverse=1. This extra
  multiplication check is essential: arbitrary additive isomorphisms need not
  preserve units. m,n positive coprime, including1.
- **ex:commutator:** conjugation sends [a,b] to [gag^-1,gbg^-1], hence preserves
  the subgroup generated by them. A quotient commutes iff every commutator dies,
  equivalent to G'<=N. For S3, sign kills commutators while [(12),(23)]=(132)
  generates A3, so S3'=A3. Trivial/abelian groups give G'={e}.
- **ex:quaternion:** matrix realization gives associative eight-element group;
  i,j,k have square-1, ij=k=-ji. Subgroups: {1}, {+/-1}, <i>,<j>,<k>, Q8.
  Order4 subgroups have index2, center +/-1 is normal. Every nontrivial subgroup
  contains-1, so two nontrivial internal factors never have trivial intersection;
  a trivial factor would force the other to be the whole group. No proper split.
- **ex:affine:** (a,b)(a',b')=(aa',ab'+b); inverse=(a^-1,-a^-1 b). Evaluate0,1
  to distinguish p(p-1) maps. Translation kernel and multiplier complement have
  trivial intersection/full product; conjugation acts by b->ab. p3 embeds as all
  six permutations of three points. p2 reduces to C2.
- **ex:normalnormal:** <s> normal in V4=<s,r²>, V4 index2 normal inD4, but
  r s r^-1=sr² lies outside<s>. Characteristic K in normal N is preserved by
  every conjugation restriction. For finite cyclic N use the unique subgroup of
  each order. For infinite cyclic N, automorphisms take generator g to g or g^-1,
  hence preserve every <g^m>. Infinite case must not use uniqueness by order.
- **ex:order8:** order8 element gives C8. If all nonidentity orders2, commute
  by C1 ex:square and choose three independent involutions to get C2³. Otherwise
  choose a of order4, b outside<a>; <a> index2 normal, b² in<a>, conjugation sends
  a to a or a^-1. In the commuting case b²=1 or a², since b4=1; replace b by
  a^-1 b in the second case, yielding C4xC2. In the inverting case b commutes
  with b²=a^k but inverts a, forcing k=-kmod4, so b²=1 or a², yielding D4 orQ8.
  Eight unique forms a^i b^epsilon and their multiplication prove the asserted
  identifications without assuming presentation theory. Distinguish by element
  order8, commutativity/exponent, and the counts5 versus1 of involutions.
