# Final editorial review

The reconstruction implements the requested ten-chapter architecture while using
the existing manuscript as its primary material. It adds a real proof-language
foundation, separates counting from probability, establishes real analytic
prerequisites earlier, and repairs the internal linear-algebra order. It preserves
the original historical openings, visual explanations, workshops, graded problems,
separate hints, discovery/write-up solutions and challenge outlooks.

The major gain is a more honest learning path. A definition now gives a reader a
task to perform, not just terminology to recognise. Finite identities are separated
from limiting claims. Maps precede coordinate calculations. Error estimates carry
their hypotheses and remainders. Finite probability is constructed before infinite
waiting examples receive a deliberately small, proved extension.

The most significant mathematical repairs are documented individually in
MATHEMATICAL-CORRECTIONS.md. The separate skeptical review found actual defects
in drafts which compilation alone could not reveal: incorrect external chapter
links, premature derivative rules, ambiguity in the elementary-function contract,
isolated-point derivative definitions, and stale forward promises. These were fixed.
The last multiplicity pointer reported in that review is now explicitly
`prop:rootsfactor(c)` and has compiled successfully. Earlier “action required”
entries in the reviewer’s chronological findings describe the first drafts;
the final integration status supersedes them.

There are 155 exercises, including every one of the 130 original identifiers.
The 25 additions consist of 18 proof-language exercises, four counting problems,
one operator bridge and two probability bridges. Ten selected bridge problems
are distributed across Chapters 4--9, drawing largely on excellent existing
problems rather than duplicating their ideas. A final investigation combines
counting, matrix powers, eigenvalues, probability and asymptotics, while showing
why a plausible rotation quotient is invalid.

The finite-difference and generating-function material, complex/polynomial tools,
and arithmetic-probability bridge prepare a reader for Volume 1 without attempting
to teach later abstract algebra, measure theory or analytic number theory here.
Some demanding original problems remain as explicitly signposted return problems.
Advanced challenge notes remain optional outlooks with references; they are not
presented as elementary results proved in full when only a sketch is supplied.

The size is deliberately governed by mathematical need. The final measurements
are in COMPILATION-AND-TESTS.md. The reconstruction does not add filler to reach a
page quota or omit substantial proofs to force a target. Its elementary entry
level and demanding optional material require the differentiated reading paths
now printed in the preface.

This is a substantive, reviewed local revision, not a certification of perfection.
The explicit follow-up limits are in OUTSTANDING-ISSUES.md: coordinated numeric
citations in other volumes, comprehensive historical proofread, physical proof,
and device-specific proofing. The subsequent EPUB/HTML validation is recorded
in DIGITAL-VALIDATION.md. None is silently counted as a completed
check. The final PDF/source deliverables, recoverable batch tags and test logs make
the revision concrete and reviewable.
