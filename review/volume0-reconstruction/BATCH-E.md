## Changes implemented

Revised complex numbers, polynomials and linear algebra in their new positions.
Introduced complex convergence after real convergence and defined complex
exponentials consistently from the admitted real functions. Added a compact
polynomial gcd/Bézout toolkit. Established maps, kernel/image, rank-nullity and
change of basis before matrix arguments; orthogonal projection/Gram--Schmidt
now precedes spectral theory. Added explicit vector-space axioms, quadratic
forms and positive semidefiniteness.

## Mathematical improvements

Repaired polygon/circulant and root-counting domain cases, polynomial
normalisation language, minimax uniqueness and Hilbert conditioning arguments.
Removed an overstatement about the trace obstruction and quantum mechanics.
Elementary root-factor and monic-division arguments eliminate core forward
dependencies into the polynomial chapter. Rolle refers to its already proved
Chapter 3 result, not an extra foundational assumption.

## Pedagogical improvements

Worked examples distinguish a map from its matrix, a basis from coordinates,
and a semidefinite form from a definite one. One new translation-operator bridge
connects finite differences with nilpotence and diagonalisation. Existing
interpolation and circulant problems remain useful bridges; a proposed new
integer-valued-polynomial problem was rejected as redundant with existing work.
