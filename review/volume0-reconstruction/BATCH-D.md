unbounded set. Limit negation makes quantifier order explicit. Derivatives are

finite integrated identity and an explicit bound, avoiding premature Taylor.
Corrected two harmonic-sum decimals. Interchanges needed later are proved from
nonnegative finite rectangle sums, not deferred as a hidden assumption.
