# Theorem verification

The separate skeptical pass covers all 81 original numbered theorems, propositions, lemmas and corollaries and both added results: 83 total, each with a proof. See theorem-verification.json for source location, statement/proof hashes and specific checks, and review-notes.txt for the editable reading notes.

This was a second statement-to-proof reading by the same editor, not an external mathematician or a proof assistant. Mathematical authorship and verification occurred in separate passes, but they are not organizationally independent. Finite computations are supporting evidence, not proofs of universal assertions.

Particular attention was given to both reciprocity proofs; Chebyshev/Bertrand thresholds; continued-fraction best-approximation metrics and periodicity; four-square descent; exceptional Eisenstein factors and units; the monic-divisor proof; conjugate ideal identities before cancellation/factorization; real unit discreteness; class-number-two bounds; maximal versus principal ideals; odd-prime and 2 splitting; p-adic field/completeness; Newton uniqueness and Ostrowski's two cases. Each has a specific ledger entry rather than a blanket success grade.

Foundational results used from Volume 0 are listed in DEPENDENCIES.md. Advanced outlooks remain expressly quoted. No formal verification, new graduate theorem proof, or fully independent historical audit is claimed.
