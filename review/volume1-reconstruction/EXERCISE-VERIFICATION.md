# Exercise, hint and solution verification

All 97 original exercises survive, with one hint and one solution each. Four new triples yield 101 total. Every solution retains How to find it and Write-up. exercise-verification.json records locations, hashes, links and a specific statement/solution check for every exercise; exercise-review-notes.txt is its source.

The review checked domains, available prerequisites, exact conclusions, hint validity, strategy, proof completeness and exceptional cases. It found and repaired the signed digit statement, early Fermat dependency, inert ideal norm, zero exponential boundary and several implicit domains. Ratings changed for the infinite-fraction construction, quadratic Fibonacci-unit problem and sqrt(10) irreducibility/class-number problem because their multi-step reasoning exceeds a technical warm-up.

Eight bridge exercises are identified in the preface. Existing coprime density, Lucas, carries, continued-fraction Bezout and Gaussian-representation problems are retained as bridges. New material adds residue covariance, norm matrices, real-unit norm signs and finite random valuation, rather than many repetitive drills.

Mathematical checks: 32,298 assertions in check_math.py and 2,965 in check_new.py (35,263 total). These include exact symbolic identities, finite parameter sweeps, Gauss sums in cyclotomic quotients, independent ideal-lattice products, every plotted Gaussian/Eisenstein prime and all forty continued-fraction quality samples. They do not prove every exercise merely by testing examples; full solutions were also read.
