# Batch F — Diophantine methods

## Changes implemented
Added a section of worked congruence, valuation and factorization methods, including failure/insufficiency cases and a complete bounded factor-pair classification. Added a discovery workshop that reconstructs the Markov descent, critiques a false jumping argument and explains the degree limitation of the conic method.

## Mathematical improvements
All new examples declare domains, handle zero before valuations and distinguish necessary congruences from existence proofs. The valuation obstruction includes its integer exponent hypothesis. Descent review checks the positive decreasing measure, coprimality of the squared factors and reversibility of Markov moves. The conic theorem retains its irreducibility/no-line-component hypothesis.

## Pedagogical improvements
The chapter now teaches how to choose and abandon tools, with actual successes and counterexamples. The workshop works backwards from a desired inequality and explains why minimizing a suitable quantity is part of discovering a proof.

## Content preservation
All original theorems, examples, figures and seven exercise triples retained. No generic recipe replaces the existing substantial arguments. Original labels remain intact.
