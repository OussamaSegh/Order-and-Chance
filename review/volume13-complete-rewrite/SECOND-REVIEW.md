## Tate’s pole sentence

Theorem `thm:tate`, lines 432–433 of `ch09-tate.tex`, now says that a trivial character has only possible poles at \(s=1\) and \(s=0\), each at most simple, with residues \(\hat f(0)\) and \(-f(0)\).

Equation `eq:tatesplit` writes the continuation as two entire integrals plus
\[
\delta_\chi\Bigl(\frac{\hat f(0)}{s-1}-\frac{f(0)}{s}\Bigr).
\]
The residues are exactly the ones in the theorem. A vanishing residue removes the pole, so “possible” and “at most simple” are the statements the formula supports. The functional-equation check later in the same proof reproduces the same two terms. Corollary `cor:riemannFE` still states genuine simple poles, because its Gaussian has \(f(0)=\hat f(0)=1\).

The only deleted lines in `volume13-arithmetic` relative to `480cbe19` are the two lines of the old sentence. `check_preservation.py --final` passes.

## Workflow

`.github/workflows/volume13-rewrite-audit.yml` triggers on `pull_request` only, with `contents: read`, no secrets, and a path filter. It checks out the pull-request head. It does not edit `build.yml` or the publication job.

`check_preservation.py` compares formal blocks, exercises, figures, labels, hints, and solutions with the frozen commit, and it allows only the Tate sentence above. `check_figures.py` checks that each of the 34 plates is referenced once. `check_new_examples.py` recomputes selected integers. Its own header says that those checks do not certify proofs. Several of its assertions are short arithmetic identities. SR-04 was the one assertion that locked a wrong normalisation; that assertion now expects the chapter’s discriminant \(4\).

The original seventeen scripts in `review/volume13-rewrite/check_ch*.py` were run with `/usr/bin/python3` after installing PARI. Each printed `all checks pass`.
